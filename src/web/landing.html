Tournaments

txapela runs the pelota tournaments of a club, from the first registration to the final, and publishes the results.

REST /api/v1/
Why txapela

One tournament, start to finish

Teams register in series, play their pools, then meet in a final phase. The tournament routes arrive step by step: today, txapela answers its health route.

📝

Written by organizers

The organizers of the club enter the teams, the courts and the scores. Nothing is scraped from a results site.

🏆

Public results

Pools, brackets and results are meant for everyone, while the contact details of the players stay with the organizers.

🗄️

A database of its own

Tournament data lives in the pilotariak-txapela D1 database, apart from the league data of Frontis. See ADR-0001.
